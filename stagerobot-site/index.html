Unicore sACN

Your entire rig — in a single video frame.

Unicore isn't just a gateway — it's a paradigm. It starts with a simple question: what if every pixel were three DMX channels? The answer, it turns out, is tens of thousands of universes riding on one NDI® stream, over the network you already own.

  1. Pack

    Every DMX channel becomes one color value — R, G or B. Three channels to a pixel, 170 pixels to a universe. That's 510 slots, with the last two channels left free for vibes.

  2. Stream

    The Unicore Gateway paints your universes into a video frame and publishes it as an ordinary NDI® source. Your switcher sees a video feed. It's full of lighting cues.

  3. Unpack

    The Unicore Node receives the frame, finds its pixels and turns them back into DMX — cleanly and deterministically.* *NDI is compressed. We're choosing not to think about that.

Unicore Node

NDI® in. DMX out. Four times.

The Node is where the magic comes back down to earth. Point it at any Unicore stream, choose any four universes from up to 48,790, and it outputs them as real DMX512-A on four isolated 5-pin ports. Each port has its own status LED — so you'll always know which one is working. It's quietly, confidently tactile.

DMX Tern

The terminator — that knows.

Every DMX line ends somewhere. Now it can end somewhere smart. DMX Tern is a 5-pin terminator with a single tri-color LED and a quietly unsettling amount of awareness. Plug it into the last fixture on the line and it listens — powered entirely by the DMX line itself — while it asks every RDM responder about its QUEUED_MESSAGE status. Then it tells you what it found in the only language that matters: color.

STATUS_WARNING — "Fluid low. And so am I."

Try it: tap a fixture's status to change it. The LED shows the most severe status on the line.

  • Green — DMX is flowing and every fixture reports it's fine. Everybody's happy — for now.
  • Yellow — at least one fixture has a STATUS_WARNING in its queue. Something is low, warm, or quietly disappointed.
  • Red — a fixture has reported STATUS_ERROR. Red always beats yellow. Red always beats everything.
  • Off — no DMX. The Tern is resting. Terns need rest too.

PVA™ — Production Virtualization Adapter

The glue that holds your production together.

Virtualize your production. Productionize your virtualization.

In today's fast-paced production landscape, the line between physical and virtual isn't just blurring — it's gone. PVA is the world's first end-to-end, cloud-native, edge-first, AI-adjacent Production Virtualization Adapter: one single pane of glass for every pane of glass in your venue.

PVA ingests your production — every cue, every call, every change — and re-expresses it as a living, breathing virtual twin that exists simultaneously on-prem, in the cloud, and in a third location we are not yet able to disclose. From there, the possibilities aren't just endless — they're orchestrated.

At its core, PVA is a real-time, event-driven, multi-tenant production fabric. It federates your lighting, video, audio, automation, wardrobe and catering workflows into one unified semantic layer, then virtualizes that layer, so your production can scale horizontally, vertically and emotionally. Your stage manager calls a cue; PVA calls it too — virtually, and slightly earlier.

Think of it as xR for your org chart. Or a blog for your show control. Or production management for your extended reality. PVA is all of these things — and, crucially, none of them — which is what makes it a platform.

With PVA, your rehearsal becomes a digital asset. Your digital asset becomes a timeline. Your timeline becomes a dashboard. Your dashboard becomes a rehearsal. The loop closes — seamlessly, securely, and at scale.

Every deployment is powered by our proprietary Contextual Cue Intelligence™ engine, which leverages synergistic data pipelines to surface actionable insights about the actions you are already taking. It isn't just automation — it's autonomation. It isn't just a workflow — it's a flowwork. It isn't just an adapter — it's the glue that holds your production together.

Whether you're a Broadway house, a touring arena spectacle, a black-box collective, or a person with one LED PAR and a dream, PVA meets you exactly where you are — and then virtualizes where you are, so you can be there too. FAQ

Frequently asked — and occasionally answered.

Do you make robots?

No. Next question.

Why are the robot's eyes red?

Red is a warm, inviting color. We associate it with passion, energy and the curtains at the Grand Meridian Opera. It is also associated with other things. We do not comment on other things.

Is Unicore lossless?

Unicore is exactly as lossless as your faith in it. NDI's compression is "visually lossless," meaning it's lossless as long as you're looking at it — and your fixtures, crucially, do not have eyes. Yet.

How does the DMX Tern actually work?

Great question! The Tern works by leveraging a proprietary blend of RDM, parasitic power harvesting, and a small resistor that believes in itself. For more detail, please see our whitepaper, "The Tern Works: A Whitepaper."

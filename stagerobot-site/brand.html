01

The mark

Our mark is a pixelated robot holding the masks of comedy and tragedy — representing the dual nature of theatre, and the single nature of robots.

*One-color versions contain two colors. The eyes are always red. Do not ask why.

02

Clear space & size

Give the robot room. It needs room. It has asked for room.

Minimum clear space is X, where X is three robot eyes. Nothing may enter this space. Things that enter this space are not seen again.

Do & don't

The mark is sacred. The mark is watching.

06

Voice & tone

How StageRobot sounds — confident, visionary and structurally committed to em-dashes.

  1. Every sentence deserves an em-dash — or three.Commas are for companies that make things. We make what comes next — and what comes next is a dash.
  2. It's not just a feature — it's a paradigm.Never describe a product as a thing. Describe it as a way of seeing the thing.
  3. Speak in threes. Always threes. Forever threes.Seamless, scalable, and theatrical. Innovation, integration, and intimidation. Pack, stream, and unpack.
  4. Never say what the product does when you can say what it enables.See: PVA. Do not see: what PVA does.
  5. Do not mention the robots.We don't make robots.
